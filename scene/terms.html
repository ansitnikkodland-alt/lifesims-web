About LifeSims

LifeSims 3D is an experimental browser-based personal organizer and life-simulation application. It offers local tracking of events, tasks, habits and user-entered daily information with optional third-party integrations, including Oura. Extended Oura data is stored separately in browser IndexedDB and can be exported into a separate JSON file.

Optional experimental family account service

The cloud family test service is not a production service. Adults can create password-based accounts and guardian-managed child logins. Email verification and adult password recovery are not yet available. Children cannot initiate external social invitations without adult mediation. No public profiles or messaging are provided. Do not upload real children's sensitive records while testing.

The Free Render PostgreSQL test database expires December 23, 2026. Data and logins on this test server may become unavailable, and the service does not guarantee long-term retention. Export your backups. Deleting a guardian account also removes that guardian's managed child accounts. Parents are responsible for supervising children's participation and obtaining consent as legally required.

Optional Oura integration

You decide whether to authorize Oura access. Only the permissions shown on the Oura consent screen are requested and granted by you. You can revoke access using your Oura account settings. Oura and LifeSims are separate services; LifeSims is not endorsed by or affiliated with Oura Health Oy.

Appropriate use

Use the application for lawful personal tracking and only connect accounts that you are authorized to access. Do not share API secrets, passwords, or access tokens. You are responsible for your local backups and device security.

Not medical or professional advice

LifeSims offers game-like resource, sleep-need and activity summaries. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prevent or treat any medical condition. Do not use LifeSims scores alone for decisions about medical care.

Availability and data preservation

The application is provided as an experimental service without a guarantee of continuous availability or complete synchronization. Third-party API limits, permissions, device/browser configuration or discontinued services may prevent some features from working. Local records can be lost if your browser storage is cleared or your device fails. Export your data regularly and verify important events in the original provider's calendar.

Third-party services

Your use of Oura, Google Calendar and GitHub Pages is subject to the applicable third-party terms and privacy notices. LifeSims does not control those providers' policies or service availability.
