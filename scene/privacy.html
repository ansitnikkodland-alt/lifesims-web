LifeSims 3D — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026

LifeSims 3D is a personal life-tracking web application. Oura synchronization is optional and only starts after you authorize it in your Oura account. This page describes the current browser-based version of LifeSims 3D.

Information you choose to use

You can enter daily notes, tasks and habits, events, sleep, weight, meals, steps, health-related measurements and other personal information. When you grant access to Oura, LifeSims requests the daily permission to read sleep and activity data and daily readiness, activity and sleep scores. It does not ask for your Oura password.

How information is used

Imported Oura sleep durations, step counts, energy expenditure and scores are displayed within LifeSims and used for optional personal tracking and game-like estimates of sleep need and resources. Such estimates are not medical assessments. Manually entered values have priority over automated imports.

Where data is stored and sent

The current version stores your LifeSims records locally in your browser, including imported Oura values. When connected, your browser communicates directly with Oura's authorization service and API to fetch the data you approve. Your Oura OAuth access token is held in the current browser tab's session storage and is not included in LifeSims JSON backups. The public Oura application Client ID and connection preferences may be saved in your browser.

LifeSims is hosted as a static website on GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web-request information such as an IP address under its own policies. Other optional integrations (for example, Google Calendar) involve their own permissions and services. This version does not operate a developer-controlled database to collect Oura metrics, does not sell Oura data, and does not use it for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

LifeSims records remain in your browser until you delete the browser's site data or replace them using the app's available import features. You can export a JSON backup; take care when sharing it, as it may contain personal health information. Disconnecting Oura ends the current session but does not erase previously imported LifeSims metrics. You can revoke LifeSims' access through your Oura account settings. Clearing the LifeSims site's browser storage removes the locally saved LifeSims progress, not just Oura values, so export a backup first if you want to retain anything.

Security and limitations

Use LifeSims through HTTPS on your own device, protect any exported backup files and do not disclose OAuth tokens or Client Secrets. Browser local storage is not end-to-end encrypted and may be accessible to someone who controls your device or browser profile. No web application can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated if the app's storage, integration or data-handling behavior changes. Review the effective date for updates.

Contact

For questions about LifeSims, open an issue in the LifeSims GitHub repository. For Oura account data requests, use the official Oura privacy and account tools.
