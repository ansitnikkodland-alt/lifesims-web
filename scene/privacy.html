LifeSims 3D — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 23, 2026

LifeSims 3D is a personal life-tracking web application. Oura synchronization is optional and only starts after you authorize it in your Oura account. This page describes the current browser-based version of LifeSims 3D.

Information you choose to use

You can enter daily notes, tasks and habits, events, sleep, weight, meals, steps, health-related measurements and other personal information. When you grant access to Oura, LifeSims asks permission to read Oura daily sleep and activity data, heart-rate readings, workouts, tags, sessions, SpO₂, stress, heart-health and ring-related measurements. You can grant or deny permissions through Oura. It does not ask for your Oura password.

How information is used

Imported Oura sleep durations, step counts, energy expenditure and scores are displayed within LifeSims and used for optional personal tracking and game-like estimates of sleep need and resources. Extended records for the requested recent period can be saved locally and downloaded separately as a JSON file. Such estimates are not medical assessments. Manually entered values have priority over automated imports.

Where data is stored and sent

The current version stores your LifeSims records locally in your browser, including imported Oura values. Detailed Oura records are stored separately in browser IndexedDB rather than in the standard LifeSims JSON backup. When connected, your browser communicates directly with Oura's authorization service and API to fetch the data you approve. Your Oura OAuth access token is held in the current browser tab's session storage and is not included in LifeSims JSON backups. The public Oura application Client ID and connection preferences may be saved in your browser.

LifeSims is hosted as a static website on GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web-request information such as an IP address under its own policies. Other optional integrations (for example, Google Calendar) involve their own permissions and services. Without cloud sign-in, the original local mode does not upload your journal to a LifeSims developer database. The optional test family account service, if enabled and used, stores the user-approved progress snapshot separately in Render PostgreSQL. The product does not sell data or use it for targeted advertising.

Locally managed family profiles and photographs

The optional Family Studio allows the owner of a device and people using the same browser to create separate local game profiles, including child profiles. These profiles are not online accounts, have no passwords, are not accessible from another device, and do not establish verified social connections. Anyone with access to the same browser storage may be able to switch between them.

Avatar photographs are resized in the browser and saved in the site's local browser storage. Room photographs are resized and stored in IndexedDB on the same device. These photographs are not uploaded to LifeSims servers for AI processing, and no 3D likeness is generated automatically. Care actions and family relationships are stored in the same local family catalog and affect only game estimates, not medical records.

To remove an avatar or a room photo, use the Family Studio controls. Browser site-data deletion also removes the locally stored family catalog and photos. Local-only profiles are not synchronized. The optional account test described below provides a separate opt-in, authenticated cross-device feature.

Optional cloud family accounts — experimental only

Adult users may register with a display name, email address and password. Email ownership is not independently verified in this prototype; there is no self-service recovery of a forgotten adult password. The adult can create child logins without a child's email, reset a managed child's password and delete the child profile. All social connections are private and require one-time codes: children cannot create or accept invitations without their guardian's account. The system has no public search, chat or public child profiles.

If a user explicitly enables cloud synchronization, their LifeSims progress snapshot, which may include personal notes and health-related entries, is uploaded to a Render PostgreSQL database in Frankfurt, Germany. Each account has a separate saved version with conflict checking. User-specific photos and rooms are uploaded only on separate actions. Authentication tokens are held in this browser tab's session storage; password hashes are stored on the server, not passwords in plain text. The original local browser data is retained. Data in local browser storage is not encrypted end-to-end, and anyone controlling that browser profile can potentially inspect it.

This test database expires on December 23, 2026. The free database is temporary and must not be relied upon for permanent storage or backups. Do not upload real children's medical records or sensitive personal images during testing. The legal obligations for processing minors' data, including notices and parental consent, still require assessment before public launch. This test is not a production child-safety platform.

Through the account interface you can download your progress as JSON, remove your cloud avatar or room, and permanently delete your account. Deleting the guardian account also deletes all child accounts managed by it and their server-side progress. Deletion does not erase data already exported to other devices, or copies retained in the local browser; clear those separately. Render's infrastructure may retain technical logs in accordance with its own policies.

Household pets, interactions and messaging

If you choose to sign in, your signed-in account is the game character. Only your household and contacts explicitly connected by invitation can interact in the family panels. Pets are shared among a guardian and their managed child accounts. The server records who fed or petted the cats, and when, or cleaned the shared litter box. Cat satiety is a game estimate calculated from the timestamp of the last feeding over 12 hours, not a veterinary assessment.

Guardian-configured care actions can record effects on a family member's game estimates, and credit the actor's skills and project. These game effects must not be relied on as evidence of a child's actual nutrition or medical condition. Family chat holds messages between directly connected users on the test server; messages are not end-to-end encrypted and there is no automatic content moderation or emergency response. Children cannot establish unsolicited connections; their invitations are mediated by their guardian. Adults can withdraw social connections. Do not send sensitive medical, school or identity data through this test chat.

Voluntary family location sharing

Location is off by default. A guardian must first approve location sharing for a child and the child must independently start it on their own signed-in device. Each adult independently controls their own location. During this test, the browser updates location only while the page is visible, with no guaranteed background operation. Family positions and accuracy estimates are visible only within that household. Location is never shared with ordinary friends or colleagues. On stopping sharing, the server deletes that account's latest location and location visit events. Place-arrival records are approximate, depend on GPS accuracy, and are retained for up to three days. No always-on service, push notifications, sound listening, SOS features or guaranteed meter-level accuracy is implemented. An OpenStreetMap link is offered only when the user explicitly chooses to open the coordinates; visiting it transfers the displayed coordinates to an external provider.

Retention and deletion

LifeSims records remain in your browser until you delete the browser's site data or replace them using the app's available import features. The separate detailed Oura collection store is replaced when you save a new detailed import. You can export a LifeSims JSON backup and separately download detailed Oura records. Both may contain personal health information; store the files privately. Disconnecting Oura ends the current session but does not erase previously imported LifeSims metrics. You can revoke LifeSims' access through your Oura account settings. Clearing the LifeSims site's browser storage removes the locally saved LifeSims progress, not just Oura values, so export a backup first if you want to retain anything.

Security and limitations

Use LifeSims through HTTPS on your own device, protect any exported backup files and do not disclose OAuth tokens or Client Secrets. Browser local storage is not end-to-end encrypted and may be accessible to someone who controls your device or browser profile. No web application can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated if the app's storage, integration or data-handling behavior changes. Review the effective date for updates.

Contact

For questions about LifeSims, open an issue in the LifeSims GitHub repository. For Oura account data requests, use the official Oura privacy and account tools.
